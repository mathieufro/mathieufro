# Mathieu Frohlich

Co-founder and CTO of [Embodme](https://embodme.com), where we make electronic musical instruments. For the past year I have been building [Liaison](https://liaison.sh), an agent-native business platform that now runs the company's operations: customer support, orders, logistics, invoicing, prospecting, social listening. Small team, used every day.

The conviction behind all of it: AI done well does not replace people. It takes the mechanical part of the work and gives people their time back, and what they do with that time is where the value is.

## What is here

- **[Liaison](https://github.com/liaison-sh/liaison)** ([liaison.sh](https://liaison.sh)): the agent-native business OS for small companies. Agents run support, orders, invoicing and shipping with a human approving what matters, and every organisation can build its own custom apps and dashboards on top. Runs Embodme and Spaycial in production. Self-host it today; Liaison Cloud opens soon.
- **[atelier](https://github.com/mathieufro/atelier)**: autonomous coding pipelines for VS Code. Brainstorm, spec, plan, build, fresh-eyes review, test, with a human only where it matters.
- **[atelier-cc](https://github.com/mathieufro/atelier-cc)**: the same pipelines as a Claude Code plugin, driven by one orchestrator and a handful of hooks.
- **[strobe](https://github.com/mathieufro/strobe)**: runtime debugging for LLM agents. Launch, trace, break and observe live programs over MCP, without recompiling.

Atelier and Strobe are the tools I built to ship Liaison.
